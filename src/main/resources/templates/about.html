Engineering + medical counselling

CareerCompass helps aspirants predict colleges from rank, score, category, quota, and historical cutoff data.

One platform for JEE, EAMCET, BITSAT, NEET UG and state counselling workflows.

Why this exists

Students often receive a rank but still struggle to translate it into realistic college choices. CareerCompass groups colleges into Safe, Moderate and Dream buckets so students can build a practical counselling list without reading hundreds of cutoff rows manually.
